## Variant comparison: does the source word cause the clustering?

Same-source share of each chunk's 10 nearest neighbours (full 1,024-dim vectors):

| Source | withsource, raw | nosource, raw | withsource, centred | nosource, centred | Expected at random |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Articles | 79% | 75% | 75% | 75% | 38% |
| Books | 90% | 88% | 75% | 76% | 23% |
| Videos | 88% | 84% | 70% | 70% | 39% |

Removing “Kniha / Článek / Oznámení / Video” from the context line lowers the raw
clustering by only 2–4 points, and after source-centring the two variants are
practically identical. The clustering comes from the texts themselves (style, length,
how each source writes about a topic), not from the label. Either variant works for
the next steps; centred vectors for the map, raw vectors for scoring questions.
