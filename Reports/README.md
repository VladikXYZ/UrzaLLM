## Embedding variants

The same chunks were embedded three times with different text in front of each chunk:

| Variant | Text in front of each chunk |
|---|---|
| `withsource` | context line naming the source: “Kniha Anarchokapitalismus › 10. Zdravotnictví”, “Článek „…“ (2017)”, “Video „…“ (2026; hosté: …)” |
| `nosource` | the same without the source word: “Anarchokapitalismus › 10. Zdravotnictví”, “„…“ (2017)” |
| `textonly` | nothing: only the chunk's own text |

## Findings across variants

**Source clustering (all chunks).** Same-source share of each chunk's 10 nearest
neighbours, full 1,024-dim vectors:

| Source | withsource raw | nosource raw | textonly raw | withsource centred | nosource centred | textonly centred | Random |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Articles | 79% | 75% | 70% | 75% | 75% | 74% | 38% |
| Books | 90% | 88% | 77% | 75% | 76% | 61% | 23% |
| Videos | 88% | 84% | 78% | 70% | 70% | 67% | 39% |

The source word alone mattered little (2–4 points). The rest of the context line
(titles, chapter paths, years, guests) mattered more: without it, raw clustering drops
another 5–11 points, and books after centring fall from 76% to 61%. What remains comes
from the texts themselves (style, length, how each source writes about a topic).

**Chapters as ideas (books only, raw).**

| Variant | Same-chapter neighbours | …from other sections | Median spread vs random | Chapters below 0.20 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `nosource` (chapter path in front) | 59% | 20% | 0.07 | 49 of 55 |
| `textonly` (no context line) | 37% | 10% | 0.19 | 29 of 55 |
| Random | 2% | | 1.00 | |

The chapter path roughly doubled the apparent chapter clustering. On content alone,
chapters still group (about 5× tighter than random, 18× more same-chapter neighbours
than chance) and related chapters stay closest in meaning, e.g. “8. Školství” of one
book next to “2. Školství” of the other, “AMEN: Ekonomie” next to “2. Proč selhává
centrální plánování?”. So regions of the map do correspond to ideas, and `textonly` is
the honest basis for showing it.

**Second projection (t-SNE).** The book reports also show a t-SNE map. It agrees with
UMAP on which chapters are compact and which are spread out (rank correlation 0.79 for
`nosource`, 0.71 for `textonly`), though it packs chapters less tightly (median spread
0.10 vs 0.07, and 0.31 vs 0.19). The chapter islands are a property of the embeddings,
not of UMAP.

`figures/` holds the charts used by the Markdown report and static PNGs of the maps.
The HTML files are self-contained.

**Linear projections (PCA, ICA; books, `textonly`).** No single direction dominates:
PC1 explains 4.7% and PC2 4.0%, and 37 components are needed for half the variance, so a
2D PCA map overlaps most chapters (median spread 0.49, against 0.19 for UMAP). PC1 is the
difference between the books (economics and institutions vs. freedom and obedience).
ICA with 16 components recovers the books' topics without any chapter information:
money, school and obedience, drugs and guns, the social system and healthcare, law and
courts, law enforcement, economic calculation, the state and democracy, and more. That is
independent, linear evidence that the embedding encodes the ideas as distinct directions.

**Linear projections, full dataset (`textonly`).** PC1 (6.6% of variance) is mostly the kind
of text: the source alone explains 39% of it (η² 0.39), books at one end and videos at the
other. From PC2 on the axes are topical. ICA (20 components) splits into 10 cross-source
themes (crypto, Austrian economists and Rothbard, the book campaign, drugs, non-aggression and
courts, school, economics, love and personal life, capitalism, healthcare and central
planning) and 10 format/series components (holiday-mode notices, “Gabriela a Urza”
announcements and videos, deCentrále invitations, the Slovak radio series, conference
panels, the opening stories, …). A map of two ICA themes mixes sources far more than
PC1 × PC2 or UMAP (same-source neighbours 41% / 25% / 42%, against 52% / 50% / 69% for
PCA and 70% / 67% / 82% for raw UMAP).

**Evolution over time (`textonly`, source-centred map).** Each article and video chunk was
assigned to the book section closest in meaning. The share landing in *Anarchoagorismus*
sections (personal freedom, obedience, self-development) rather than *Anarchokapitalismus*
(institutions, economics) rises from 26% (2015–2018) to 60% (2023–2026), with the change
around 2021. It shows within videos (19–28% before 2021, 41–73% after) and within articles
separately, so it is not just the switch from articles to videos. The free school “Ježek bez
klece” grows from 0% to 13%; work, roads and guns shrink.

**Topic distribution (`textonly`).** The books' 75 significant PCA directions define the
concept space; each book section's average is a concept centre (58 topical sections plus
framing ones). Book chunks held out from their section land back in it 66% of the time
(top 3: 84%; chance 1.5%). Articles and videos reach every topical section. Most covered:
the free school “Ježek bez klece”, “Svobodný přístav”, school, democracy, anarchy. Far more
present than in the books: the free school, anarchy, democracy and several AMEN objections;
far less: self-development, authority, participation, leadership. Articles lean to
*Anarchokapitalismus* sections (59%), videos to *Anarchoagorismus* (53%). Articles and videos
fit the sections less tightly than the books themselves (median 0.26 vs 0.41); there is no
natural “outside” cut-off, and the worst-fitting texts are current affairs (censorship,
Trump, EU myths) the books don't cover.

**Urza in the political space (`textonly`).** A list of politics in general, independent of Urza
(326 items, `Questions/political_space.tsv`), embedded with the same model on this machine
(identical to the GPU vectors). 92% of the questions have at least a relevant Urza passage
(similarity ≥ 0.55) and 55% a strongly matching one (≥ 0.60); spot checks find exactly his
texts on guns, taxes as theft or who would build roads. By volume he concentrates on the state
and public administration, education, law and justice, and civil liberties; the thinnest areas,
and the blind spots, are energy, the EU and international relations, agriculture, housing and
municipalities.

**Trails between book topics (`textonly`).** Two sections are linked when their passages are
each other's nearest neighbours more often than chance (208 links, lift ≥ 2). The backbone (63
strongest links) and the paths follow the books' lines of argument: school → indoctrination →
democracy; money → cryptocurrency → crypto-anarchy → agora; drugs → smugglers and dealers →
liberation → future → love. The hubs are the broad, summarising sections (Budoucnost, Participace,
Boření mýtů, Revoluce, Závěr); 7 backbone links join the two books (money–cryptocurrency,
drugs–smugglers, school–school, non-aggression–crime, …).

**Nearest topics in the books (`textonly`).** Each of the 56 book topics points to the topic
closest in meaning (average vectors, full space). Following the arrows gives 10 families, each
ending in a mutual pair: economics (2. Proč selhává centrální plánování? ↔ AMEN: Ekonomie), law
(3. Princip neagrese ↔ 14. Soudnictví), the state (8. Stát ↔ 10. Etatismus, reached from drugs
and guns), school (8. Školství ↔ 2. Školství, reached from indoctrination), money, obedience and
leadership, and a large personal-freedom family ending in 21. Budoucnost ↔ AMEN: aforismy. The
nearest topic in meaning is the nearest on the map for only 18% of topics.
