Ideas over time · source-centred UMAP, variant textonly

Where in the space of ideas was Urza in each year? The map is the same source-centred UMAP map as in the textonly projection report. Move the slider (or press play) to light up one year's articles and videos. The books stay on the map as a fixed reference: they carry no year in the data (both editions were published in 2026), but their chapters name the regions of the map.

What was done

  1. Map. The coordinates are those of the source-centred UMAP map of all chunks (textonly embeddings, each source's average vector subtracted), so this map matches the one in the projection report.
  2. Concept regions. Every article and video chunk was given the region of the book section closest to it in meaning: its 5 most similar book chunks (source-centred vectors) vote, weighted by similarity. There are 65 regions: the book chapters, with the appendix and AMEN split into their topics and the broad “19. Praxe” split into its subsections. Region labels sit at the average position of the book chunks.
  3. Years. Articles and videos carry their publication year (2015–2026). Announcements (short event notices) are shown but left out of all yearly numbers. For each year, the black trail marks the average position of its chunks: the year's centre of gravity.

How the focus moved

Shares of concept regions in the first years (2015–2018, 592 chunks) against the last years (2023–2026, 556 chunks), articles and videos together, announcements excluded.

  • Grew the most: “Ježek bez klece” (0% → 13%), “Doslov” (0% → 3%), “14. Politika” (0% → 3%), “15. Osvobození” (1% → 3%), “5. Adultismus” (0% → 2%).
  • Shrank the most: “Tři příběhy” (10% → 2%), “Práce” (5% → 1%), “8. Školství” (6% → 3%), “12. Silnice a dopravní pravidla” (4% → 1%), “Zbraně” (5% → 2%).
  • From one book's ideas towards the other's. Share of chunks whose nearest book section is in Anarchoagorismus (personal freedom, obedience, self-development) rather than Anarchokapitalismus (institutions, economics): 26% in 2015–2018 against 60% in 2023–2026.

Reading it: the regions that grew are those of the free school Urza co-founded (“Ježek bez klece”) and of the personal-freedom chapters of Anarchoagorismus; the ones that shrank are the institutional topics of Anarchokapitalismus (work, roads, guns) and the stories, which early narrative blog posts resembled. The shift towards Anarchoagorismus happens around 2021 and appears within articles and within videos separately, so it is not just the move from articles to videos that happened at the same time.

Caveats

  • The source mix changes over time. Until 2020 most dated chunks are articles; from 2021 most are videos (see the volume chart). The map is source-centred, which removes most of the difference in how the two sources embed, but part of any change can still be a change of format rather than of subject.
  • Videos are represented by their descriptions, not by what is said in them, so their region reflects the summary Urza wrote.
  • Regions are the nearest book section, not a topic label. A chunk about something the books do not cover still gets the closest section (hover shows how close: the best similarity to a book chunk). Narrative or personal posts tend to land near the books' stories and personal chapters.
  • Uncertain years: none. The last year (2026) is incomplete.
