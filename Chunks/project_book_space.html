The book topics as a space · articles, video descriptions and transcripts placed among the books' concepts

Every chapter heading and subheading of Anarchokapitalismus and Anarchoagorismus is one concept. The map is built from the books alone; everything Urza wrote or said elsewhere is then placed into it, by which book topics it is closest to.

What was done

  • Concepts: Chapter numbers are removed (“10. Healthcare” → “Healthcare”), and a name that appears in both books (“Education”, “Cryptomoney”) is one concept. A concept's vector is the average of the book paragraphs under that heading (a chapter includes its subsections), so it stands for what the book actually says there, not just for its title.
  • The space: every chunk is described by its similarity (cosine) to each of the concepts. Each concept's similarities are standardised on the book paragraphs, and each chunk's profile is centred, so only which topics a chunk leans towards counts, not how book-like it is overall (transcripts are less similar to every book topic, simply because speech is not book prose).
  • The map is UMAP (cosine, 15 neighbours, min_dist 0.1, seed 42) fitted on the book paragraphs only. The articles, video descriptions and transcripts are then placed into that finished map (umap.transform), so they cannot change it. Each concept label sits at the average position of its paragraphs.
  • Source-centred: the same after subtracting each source's average vector first, removing what a source's chunks share just by being articles, descriptions, transcripts or book prose.
  • Data: Both models (bge-m3 and EmbeddingGemma, embedded on the GPU from the Czech text); EmbeddingGemma's map is rotated and scaled onto bge-m3's using the book paragraphs, which does not change its shape.
