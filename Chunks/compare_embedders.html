bge-m3 vs EmbeddingGemma · the same 2,911 chunks in two embedding spaces

Urza's articles, video descriptions and book paragraphs (real_data/), embedded by both models from the same Czech text. How differently do the two models arrange them?

What was done

  • Data: real_data/chunked.json: One chunk per article text, per video description (cleaned of donation boilerplate) and per book paragraph.
  • Vectors: real_data/embeddings_out_cs/, embedded on the GPU from the Czech text alone: BAAI/bge-m3 (1,024 numbers per chunk) and EmbeddingGemma (embeddinggemma_2, 768 numbers). Checked: same row order as the chunks, no missing values, all re-normalised to length 1.
  • Maps: UMAP (cosine distance, 15 neighbours, min_dist 0.1, seed 42) of each model, of the raw vectors and of the source-centred vectors (each source's average vector subtracted, so a shared “this is a video description” direction cannot pull chunks together). EmbeddingGemma's map is rotated, mirrored and scaled to fit bge-m3's as closely as possible (Procrustes), so the same regions sit in the same place; this does not change its shape.
  • Measurements use the full vectors, not the maps: each chunk's 10 nearest neighbours by cosine similarity; how many are from the same source (or book chapter); how many neighbours the two models share; and the rank correlation of cosine similarities over 200,000 random pairs of chunks.
