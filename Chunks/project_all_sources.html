All sources on one map · raw vs source-centred · bge-m3 and EmbeddingGemma

Urza's articles, book paragraphs, video descriptions and, new, the transcripts of what is said in the videos, projected together. Does each kind of text form its own territory, and what is left once the “kind of text” is removed?

What was done

  • Data (real_data/):
  • Vectors: both models embedded the Czech text of every chunk on the GPU (embeddings_out_cs/, embeddings_out_trans_cs/); bge-m3 gives 1,024 numbers per chunk, EmbeddingGemma 768. All were checked against the chunk texts (same order) and normalised to length 1.
  • Raw map: UMAP (cosine distance, 15 neighbours, min_dist 0.1, seed 42) of all chunks together.
  • Source-centred map: the same after subtracting each source's average vector from its chunks (four averages: articles, books, descriptions, transcripts). Whatever all transcripts share just because they are spoken language, or all book paragraphs because they are book prose, is removed; what is left is the topic.
  • EmbeddingGemma's maps are rotated, mirrored and scaled to match bge-m3's (Procrustes), so the same regions sit in the same place; this does not change their shape.
