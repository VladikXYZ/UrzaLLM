Book idea map · raw embeddings, variant nosource

Do regions of the embedding map correspond to ideas? The two books are the cleanest test: their chapters are topic labels written by the author. This report maps only the book chunks, with the raw embeddings (no source-centring, which is unnecessary when all chunks come from one source).

What was done

  1. Data. The 592 book chunks (about 150 words each) from Anarchokapitalismus and Anarchoagorismus, with their bge-m3 embeddings (variant nosource: the context line is “Anarchokapitalismus › 10. Zdravotnictví” etc., without the word “Kniha”).
  2. Chapter groups. Each chunk belongs to one of 56 groups: a chapter, or in the appendix of Anarchokapitalismus its own topic (Drogy, Zbraně, Věda, Práce) or AMEN category (Ekonomie, Historie, …). All AMEN aphorisms of Anarchoagorismus form one group.
  3. Map. UMAP to 2D (cosine distance, 15 neighbours, min_dist 0.1, fixed seed) on these chunks only. Each chapter's name is placed at the average position of its chunks.
  4. Measurements. Who are each chunk's 10 nearest neighbours (full 1,024-dim vectors), and how tightly each chapter's chunks sit together on the map.

Who are a chunk's nearest neighbours?

How it is computed: for each book chunk, the 10 most similar book chunks (cosine similarity of the full vectors), and the share of them that come from the same section, the same chapter group, or the same book. Expected at random is the share if neighbours were picked blindly.

Reading it: 59% of a chunk's nearest neighbours are from its own chapter, against 2% by chance. Part of that is trivial: 48% are from the very same section (neighbouring paragraphs, which also share one overlapping sentence). Looking only at neighbours from other sections, 20% are still from the same chapter group, i.e. different parts of a chapter find each other. 92% of neighbours are from the same book (50% by chance): the two books differ in subject and tone.

Reading it: the median chapter has a spread ratio of 0.07, i.e. its chunks are about 14× more concentrated than random; 49 of 55 groups are below 0.20. The loosest groups are the broad ones: 19. Praxe (0.94), XX. Boření mýtů (0.65), AMEN: Ekonomie (0.34), 20. Participace (0.32), 12. Anarchie (0.31). “19. Praxe” is nine different subsections (Ježek bez klece, Svobodný přístav, Kryptoanarchie, Agora, …), and “XX. Boření mýtů” debunks myths from many areas, so they are spread because their content is.

Conclusions

  • Yes: regions of the map correspond to ideas. In the books, almost every chapter occupies its own compact region, and chapters that discuss related subjects lie next to each other. This is the evidence that the embedding organises Urza's texts by topic, which the coverage map relies on.
  • Where a chapter is spread out, its content is mixed. The loose groups are exactly the ones that cover several subjects, not failures of the embedding.
  • Caveat: the chapter name is in the text. Every chunk starts with its chapter path (“Anarchokapitalismus › 10. Zdravotnictví”), which helps chunks of one chapter find each other. The cross-section figure (20%) and the fact that related chapters sit side by side suggest the content does most of the work, but the effect of the name is not measured separately here.
  • The books are a good “skeleton” for the full map. Their chapter labels can name the regions of the full map (articles and videos included), since the same topics recur across sources.
