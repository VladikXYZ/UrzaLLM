Trails between the books' topics · variant textonly

On the book map, sections sit next to related sections, and following those neighbours reads like a line of argument, for example 2. Školství → 3. Indoktrinace → 9. Demokracie. This report turns those neighbourhoods into a graph of links between the 65 book sections, shows its backbone, and lets you find the chain of strongest links between any two topics, with the passages that bridge each step.

What was done

  1. Links from the text itself. For every book chunk (embedded from its own text only), its 10 most similar book chunks were taken. Every time a neighbour belongs to a different section, that counts as one link between the two sections.
  2. Strength = lift: the number of links divided by the number expected if neighbours were picked at random (bigger sections would get more links by chance). Lift 10 = ten times more often than chance. Only links with lift ≥ 2 and at least 3 neighbour pairs are kept: 208 links.
  3. Backbone: the maximum spanning tree, the 63 strongest links that connect all sections without loops. It is the skeleton of the trails.
  4. Paths: between two sections, the chain whose links are strongest overall (each step costs 1 / lift, and the cheapest chain wins). For every step, the most similar pair of passages across it is shown: the actual “bridge” between the two topics.
  5. Positions are the section centres on the textonly book map (same UMAP as the book map report). The opening stories were moved closer on the map, as in the book map report. Distances on the map are only a guide; the links are computed in the full space.

Conclusions

  • The books' topics form connected trails, not isolated islands; only “Tři příběhy” has no strong link. Chains such as 2. Školství → 3. Indoktrinace → 9. Demokracie; 5. Peníze → Kryptopeníze → Kryptoanarchie → Agora → Doslov → 21. Budoucnost → 16. Neposlušnost; Drogy → Pašeráci, dealeři, děvky a další svobodní psanci → 15. Osvobození → 21. Budoucnost → 22. Láska link related topics step by step, found from the text alone.
  • The hubs are broad or central sections: “21. Budoucnost” (17 links), “20. Participace” (15 links), “XX. Boření mýtů” (13 links), “13. Revoluce” (13 links), “Závěr; a co dál?” (12 links). They draw together themes from across the books, so many trails pass through them; the specific chapters sit at the ends of trails, with few links (“5. Peníze” 3, “Drogy” 3, “8. Školství” 4; the median section has 7).
  • For UrzaLLM: the trails are a ready-made structure for the reasoning-tree interface. A debate that starts at one topic can follow the strongest links to the next argument, citing the bridging passages along the way.
  • Caveat: a link means the passages are similar in content, not that the author argues from one to the other; the direction of a trail is the reader's.
